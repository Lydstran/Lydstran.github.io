About Me

Hello! I am Lydia Stranathan, a photographer based in Chapel Hill, NC. I am also a Master's in City and Regional Planning student at UNC-Chapel Hill.

I started taking photos in high school and loved getting to take people's senior photos. I would describe my style as a mix of candid and posed, with a clean and timeless editing style.

In my free time, I enjoy traveling, spending time outdoors, cooking/baking, and learning how to make websites like this one!
